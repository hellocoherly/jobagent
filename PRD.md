# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Project:** Agentic Career
**Working Name:** Compass AI (working title)

**Tagline:** Discover where your career belongs.

## Vision Statement

Build the world's first AI-native Career Operating System that autonomously represents professionals throughout their careers. Rather than helping users search for jobs, Compass AI continuously understands the individual, monitors the market, discovers opportunities, evaluates fit, and assists users in making informed career decisions.

## Executive Summary

Today's job search is fundamentally reactive. Professionals spend hours searching job boards, uploading resumes, and filling out repetitive applications.

Compass AI transforms job search into an agentic career management experience.

Each user has an AI Career Agent that:

- Understands who they are professionally
- Continuously monitors the job market
- Discovers opportunities
- Evaluates employers
- Explains recommendations
- Prepares applications
- Learns from interactions
- Represents the user throughout their career

The application is centered around an interactive Career Map that visualizes employers, opportunities, professional networks, and career intelligence.

## Product Vision

Become the operating system for professional careers.

Instead of asking:

> "What jobs are available?"

Users ask:

> "What's the smartest next step in my career?"

## Core Product

### AI Career Agent

Every user receives an AI agent.

Responsibilities:

#### Understand

Maintain a living professional profile including:

- Skills
- Experience
- Projects
- Achievements
- Career goals
- Preferred industries
- Work style
- Compensation expectations
- Commute preferences
- Geographic preferences
- Learning history
- Certifications

#### Discover

Continuously monitor:

- Job postings
- Employer hiring
- Recruiter outreach
- Internal mobility (future)
- Startup opportunities
- Fractional work
- Advisory roles
- Executive positions

#### Evaluate

Score every opportunity.

Dimensions include:

- Professional Fit
- Compensation
- Growth Potential
- Culture
- Leadership
- Industry
- Company Stability
- Commute
- Remote flexibility
- Cost of Living
- Promotion likelihood
- Team quality
- Manager reputation (where available)
- Professional network
- Future skill growth

#### Recommend

Instead of notifications:

Explain recommendations.

Example:

> This role increases compensation by approximately 15%, reduces commute by 20 minutes, aligns with your leadership goals, and strengthens your AI product management experience.

#### Learn

Continuously improve recommendations using:

- Applications
- Interview feedback
- Offer outcomes
- Career progression
- User interactions

## Long-Term Vision

Compass AI is not a job board.

It is a Career Operating System.

Just as Google Maps became the default way to navigate the physical world, Compass AI aims to become the default way professionals navigate their careers.

The AI Career Agent works continuously—understanding the user, monitoring the market, evaluating opportunities, and surfacing the next best career move. Users no longer "look for jobs"; they make informed career decisions with an intelligent agent acting as their trusted representative. Over time, the platform evolves into the central hub for career growth, employer discovery, networking, relocation, compensation planning, and lifelong professional development.
